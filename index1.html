💧 Part 1 · What is Water?

What is Water?

🚰

Water is a clear liquid. It has no colour, no smell and no taste.

✨ Big Word: WATERA clear liquid that all living things — people, animals and plants — need to live.

We use water every day for:

🥤Drinking
🍳Cooking
🛁Bathing

Why Do We Need Water?

Water is like a superpower for staying alive! 💪

🫀We drink water to stay healthy and full of energy.
🌱Animals and plants need water to grow too.
🐟Many animals like fish live in water!

The Three Magic Forms of Water

Water can change into three forms. Tap each one! 👇

🧊SOLIDIce — hard & cold
💧LIQUIDWater we drink & pour
💨GASWater vapour — floats in air, we can't see it!

🏔️ Part 4 · Where Water Comes From

Where Does Water Come From?

🏔️🌧️

Water comes from rain and melting snow on the mountains. It fills up rivers, lakes, ponds and seas. Some water sinks deep into the soil — we call that groundwater.

🔄 Part 6 · The Water Cycle

The Amazing Water Cycle

Water never sits still — it keeps moving in a never-ending journey! Tap Next Step to follow Dewy. 💧

1☀️Evaporation: The warm Sun heats the water. It turns into water vapour and rises up into the sky.
2☁️Condensation: High in the cold sky, vapour cools. Tiny drops join to make fluffy clouds.
3🌧️Rain (Precipitation): The clouds get heavy and the drops fall down as rain, snow or hail.
4🌊Collection: Rain flows back into rivers, lakes and seas… then the Sun heats it again. Round and round!
🧼 Part 7 · Keep Water Clean

Keep Our Water Clean

💖Clean water keeps us healthy and strong.
🚮Throwing waste or bathing in rivers makes water dirty.
🤒Drinking dirty water can make us ill — so we must save and protect clean water!
