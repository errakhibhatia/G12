All About AIR

🎈 A Sky Adventure for Super Kids · Class 2 & 3

Take a big breath in… and let it out. Whoosh! 🌬️

Do you know what you just breathed? It was AIR! Air is everywhere — but it likes to play hide-and-seek, because we cannot see it!

🔍We cannot see air, we cannot smell clean air, and we cannot hold it in our hands. But air is real — it is all around you right now!
🌬️ Part 1 · What is Air?

So… what is air?

Air is a kind of gas. A gas has no shape and no colour — it spreads out to fill every empty space, like a balloon filling up.

✨ Big Word: AIR Air is the invisible gas all around us that living things need to breathe and stay alive.

Even when a glass looks empty, it is not really empty… it is full of air! Want proof?

🥤Try it at home: Push an empty glass straight down into a tub of water, upside-down. The water cannot get in! Why? Because air is already inside the glass, holding the water out. Sneaky air! 😄

Air takes up space, air has weight, and moving air can push things — that is how it moves sailboats and flies kites!

💚 Part 2 · Why do we need Air?

Why is air so important?

Air is like a superhero that helps everyone, every single second!

1. Air gives us life 🫁

People, animals, birds, fish and even plants need air to breathe. We breathe in the good part of air to live. Without air, no living thing could survive — not even for a few minutes!

2. Air does many helpful jobs 💪

🧺Dries clothesWind takes away the water
🪁Flies kitesAir pushes the kite up high
⛵Moves sailboatsWind pushes the big sails
🌻Carries seedsSo new plants can grow
🔥Helps things burnFire needs air to stay lit
🎐Makes sound travelWe hear through the air
💨Cool fact: Plants give out the fresh air that we breathe, and we give out the air that plants like. We are best friends with trees! 🌳💕 So planting trees keeps our air clean.
🧩 Part 3 · What is Air made of?

The things hiding inside air

Air is not just one thing — it is a mixture of many tiny things mixed together. Tap each one to learn its secret! 👇

🧠Clean air vs dirty air: Smoke, too much dust, and germs make the air impure (dirty). Clean fresh air keeps us healthy and happy!
🍃 Part 4 · Moving Air = Wind

When air moves, we call it WIND

Air loves to move around. When air moves, it gets a special name!

🍃 Big Word: WIND Wind is moving air. We cannot see it, but we can feel it and watch it move leaves, flags and our hair!
🌱BreezeA gentle, soft wind. Feels nice!
🌪️StormA very strong, fast wind. Be careful!

So how can we find out which way the wind is blowing? We use a clever helper… turn the page! 👉

🐓 Part 5 · The Weathercock

Meet the Weathercock!

A weathercock (also called a weather vane) is a tool shaped like a rooster 🐓 that sits high up on roofs. It spins around and points to show us which direction the wind is blowing.

🐓 Big Word: WEATHERCOCK A weathercock shows the direction of the wind — North, South, East or West. It does not tell speed!
NS EW 🐓
Tap a button to blow the wind! 💨
🧭Easy way to remember the directions: Never Eat Soggy Worms! (North, East, South, West — going around like a clock 🕐)

🫁 We need air to breathe and stay alive.
🧩 Air is made of: gases, water vapour, dust, smoke & germs.
🍃 Moving air = wind. Gentle = breeze, strong = storm.
🐓 Weathercock shows the wind's DIRECTION (not its speed!).
